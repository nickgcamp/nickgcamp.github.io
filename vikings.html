The worst Vikings QBR games since 2010

A ranked chart of the 26 worst Minnesota Vikings quarterback games by ESPN Total QBR, made the week it happened.

Problem
After a rough Sunday in November 2026 I wanted to know where it ranked.
Approach
Pulled ESPN Total QBR from game logs, kept regular-season games with at least 20 plays, ranked them with ties sharing a rank, and designed the chart so the newest game stands out.
Result
Carson Wentz against Buffalo on Nov 9, 2026 tied Matt Cassel’s 2013 game at Cincinnati for the lowest, a QBR of 2.3. It’s the start of a side project: sports graphics built from data, one team first.
